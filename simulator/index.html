What can go wrong

Hydroponics

A pump or timer failure dries out thin-film roots within hours; a backup pump and a low-flow alarm are required. Nutrient salts must be shipped from Earth. A root disease can spread through the shared solution, so racks need to be isolatable.

Aquaponics

Fish depend on dissolved oxygen: an aeration failure can kill a whole tank in hours, far faster than plants suffer. Ammonia builds up if the biofilter stalls. Feed must be imported or grown. Nobody has raised fish for food in 1/6 g yet, so swimming, feeding and waste settling are open questions.
