# Lunar Habitat Engineering Portal

**Live site:** https://jaigod.github.io/lunar-habitat-engineering/

Engineering research for the Project ARES lunar habitat (NASA HUNCH), with working calculators:

- **Water pumping systems**: storage, filtration, greywater recycling (ISS reaches ~98%), backup pumps, leak detection, and what happens when each fails.
- **Fluid dynamics laboratory**: what changes at 1/6 g. A calculator compares tank pressure, bubble rise, natural convection and pump work on the Moon and Earth.
- **Radiation protection centre**: galactic cosmic rays, solar particle events, water and regolith shielding, storm shelter. A dose calculator uses the 1,369 µSv/day measured on the lunar surface by Chang'e 4 and NASA's 600 mSv career limit.
- **Food production and aquaponics centre**: fish farming vs hydroponics only, fish species, water quality, open questions about 1/6 g.
- **[Aquaponics vs Hydroponics Simulator](https://jaigod.github.io/lunar-habitat-engineering/simulator/)**: change crew, mission length, water, grow area, crops and fish stock. See calories, protein, water, power, mass and imported feed for both systems.
- **Our websites**: links to every Project ARES site.

## How the simulator works

Crop yields and light needs come from NASA's Biomass Production Chamber. Food energy needs come from NASA's Life Support Baseline Values and Assumptions Document. Fish values (feed balance 57 g/m²/day, feed conversion 1.7, stocking 60 kg/m³) come from the University of the Virgin Islands aquaponics system. Values we estimated ourselves are tagged "our assumption" on the page. The full table, with links, is at the bottom of the simulator.

**Limits:** a simplified steady-state model for comparing options. It is not a flight design. Nobody has raised fish at lunar gravity yet.
