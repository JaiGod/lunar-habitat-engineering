Dose model: lunar surface 1,369 µSv/day (Chang'e 4). Solar storm attenuation fitted to Geant4 results for regolith (Matthiä & Berger 2024: >4 g/cm² keeps a large event under the 30-day limit, >10 g/cm² halves it again). Cosmic-ray reduction capped near 25-35%, matching the same study (180 g/cm² of regolith cut dose only ~25%). Simplified for teaching; not a transport code.
